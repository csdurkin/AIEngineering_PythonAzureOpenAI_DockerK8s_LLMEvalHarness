# Rule 2: In a series of three or more terms with a single conjunction, use a comma after each term except the last
# Category: Chapter II, Elementary Rules of Usage
# Source: Strunk, W. (1920). The Elements of Style, Chapter II. Project Gutenberg eBook 37134.

Rule 2 [Elementary Rules of Usage]. In a series of three or more terms with a single conjunction, use a comma after each term except the last. Thus write, red, white, and blue; gold, silver, or copper; He opened the letter, read it, and made a note of its contents. This is also the usage of the Government Printing Office and of the Oxford University Press. In the names of business firms the last comma is omitted, as, Brown, Shipley & Co.
